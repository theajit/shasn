# Short description

Outwit your rivals in a sharp, local multiplayer game of political strategy.

# Long description

SHASN Online brings the tension, negotiation, and tactical choices of political strategy to your Android device.

Gather 2–5 players around one screen and compete to shape the map. Answer ideology questions, gain resources, influence voters, redraw boundaries, trade with rivals, and deploy conspiracies at just the right moment. Every decision can shift the balance of power.

Key features:

- Local pass-and-play multiplayer for 2–5 players
- A private handoff screen that keeps each player's choices secret
- Original and dynamically generated board layouts
- Ideology cards with distinct powers and progression
- Strategic voter placement, trading, gerrymandering, and conspiracies
- Optional filtering for sensitive card content
- Automatic local game saving so you can continue after closing the app
- Built-in rules for quick reference

Build influence, negotiate when it helps, betray expectations when it matters, and secure enough majorities to win. No two contests unfold the same way.
