SHASN Online

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated September 19, 2026

SHASN Online ("the App") is operated by Ajit ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle

Local games

For local pass-and-play games, game state, player names, preferences, and settings are stored only on your device using local app storage. We do not receive this information.

Online games

When you create, join, or resume an online room, the App sends the following information to the room server:

  • The display name and player color you choose
  • Room codes, player identifiers, reconnect tokens, and a hashed reconnect PIN
  • Game setup choices, game state, moves, and trade proposals or responses
  • Connection information required to provide the service, which may include your IP address and basic server request or diagnostic data processed by the hosting provider

You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or use your real name to play.

How we use information

We use this information only to create and operate games, synchronize private rooms, reconnect players, maintain the service, diagnose technical problems, and comply with applicable law.

The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell personal information.

Local storage

The App stores local game progress, interface preferences, and online-room reconnect details on your device. This information remains until you leave the room, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Clearing local storage may remove saved games and prevent reconnection to an existing online room.

Online room retention

Online rooms and their game state are held in the room server's temporary memory. They are cleared when the server restarts. We do not intentionally create permanent player profiles or a permanent history of completed games. Limited technical logs may be retained by our hosting provider according to its operational and security practices.

Sharing

Player display names, colors, connection status, and relevant game state are shared with other participants in the same private room. Do not use a display name that reveals information you do not want other invited players to see.

We may also share limited information with infrastructure providers that host or deliver the online service, or when required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect users and the service. We do not sell or rent your information.

Permissions and device access

The Android App requests internet access so online rooms can function. It does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information handled by the App. Reconnect PINs are stored on the room server as salted hashes, and reconnect tokens are randomly generated. However, no network transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep room codes and reconnect credentials private.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

Your choices

You can play locally without using the online-room service. You can remove locally stored information by leaving an online room, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App. Because online rooms are temporary and no user account is created, we may be unable to identify a specific user's room data without the relevant room details.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant data.

International processing

Online-room information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on the location of our hosting infrastructure. Those countries may have different data-protection laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date on this page when we make material changes.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email ajit@theajit.in.
